# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What that means

- Critique code, not people. "This misreads Article 7(2)" is useful. "You clearly
  didn't read the regulation" is not.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about tradeoffs, not competence.
- Accept that maintainers say no. This project is maintained in spare time and
  scope is finite. A declined PR is not a judgement of you.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, publishing private
information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub](https://github.com/Vujavujavuja/Overdoo/security/advisories/new) or
by direct message. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that breach
this policy, and may block repeat offenders.
